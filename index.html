Dashavatara

"Yada yada hi dharmasya glanir bhavati bharata, abhyutthanam adharmasya tadatmanam srjamy aham"

Whenever righteousness declines and unrighteousness rises, Lord Vishnu descends to Earth in various incarnations to restore cosmic order (Dharma) across the four Yugas.

Explore the 10 Avatars

The Ten Incarnations

From aquatic life to the prophesied future warrior across Satya, Treta, Dvapara, and Kali Yugas

#01 Satya Yuga
🐟

Matsya

The Giant Fish

Vishnu took the form of a giant fish to save Manu (the first man), the seven sages, seeds of all plants, and the sacred Vedas from a catastrophic great flood.

#02 Satya Yuga
🐢

Kurma

The Cosmic Tortoise

During the churning of the cosmic ocean (Samudra Manthan) by the Devas and Asuras, Vishnu incarnated as a tortoise to balance Mount Mandara on his shell.

#03 Satya Yuga
🐗

Varaha

The Wild Boar

When the demon Hiranyaksha dragged the Earth (Bhudevi) to the bottom of the cosmic ocean, Vishnu appeared as a massive boar and lifted the Earth back on his tusks.

#04 Satya Yuga
🦁

Narasimha

The Half-Man, Half-Lion

Emerging from a pillar at twilight, this fierce form defeated the tyrant Hiranyakashipu—bypassing his boon of near-immortality—to protect his young devotee Prahlada.

#05 Treta Yuga
☂️

Vamana

The Dwarf Brahmin

Appearing as a humble dwarf priest, Vishnu asked the benevolent yet proud Asura king Mahabali for three paces of land, growing into a cosmic giant to reclaim the three worlds.

#06 Treta Yuga
🪓

Parashurama

The Warrior Sage with an Axe

Born to sage Jamadagni, Parashurama wielded a divine axe granted by Lord Shiva to rid the Earth of corrupt and tyrannical warrior kings who abused their power.

#07 Treta Yuga
🏹

Rama

The Ideal Prince of Ayodhya

The central hero of the epic Ramayana, Rama exemplified truth, duty, and ideal leadership, defeating the ten-headed demon king Ravana to rescue Sita.

#08 Dvapara Yuga
🦚

Krishna

The Divine Statesman & Charioteer

Central to the Mahabharata, Krishna guided Arjuna on the battlefield of Kurukshetra, delivering the timeless spiritual wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita.

#09 Kali Yuga
☸️

Buddha

The Enlightened Teacher

Recognized in Vaishnava tradition as the ninth incarnation, he promoted non-violence (Ahimsa), compassion toward all living beings, and liberation from suffering.

#10 End of Kali Yuga
🐎

Kalki

The Prophesied Future Warrior

Yet to appear at the end of the current dark age (Kali Yuga), Kalki is foretold to ride a white horse (Devadatta) with a blazing sword to end evil and begin a new Satya Yuga.
